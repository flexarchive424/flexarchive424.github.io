Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flex Studio. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Flex Studio application.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize Flex Studio using TikTok Login Kit, we access your basic profile information (e.g., OpenID, Avatar, Display Name) solely to identify your session. We do not store this data on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The permissions requested (video.publish, video.upload) are used exclusively to process your video files and transmit them to TikTok's servers. Flex Studio acts solely as a pass-through tool.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or video content to any outside third parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Security

All communication between Flex Studio and TikTok is encrypted via HTTPS. Videos uploaded through our tool are processed locally and sent directly to TikTok.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy related to Flex Studio, please contact us.
